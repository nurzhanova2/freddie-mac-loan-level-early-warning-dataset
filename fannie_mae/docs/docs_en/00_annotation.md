# Research abstract

## Official topic

**“Explainable artificial intelligence for early detection of credit risk and
hidden deterioration in borrower quality.”**

## Practical result

The practical result is a **SupTech-inspired prototype of an explainable
mortgage-credit early-warning system**. It converts information available at
the prediction date into an early risk score, deterioration signal, explanation
of risk drivers, and a trigger that prioritises human review.

This does not alter the official topic: Explainable AI remains the method,
early risk detection remains the task, and hidden quality deterioration remains
the target phenomenon. SupTech-inspired architecture describes the practical
use of results; it does not claim deployment as a central-bank system.
