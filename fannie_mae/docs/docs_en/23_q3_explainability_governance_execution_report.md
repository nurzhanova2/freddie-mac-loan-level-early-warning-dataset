# Stage 23. Explainability and model governance on Q3 cohorts

Global TreeSHAP explanations from the frozen XGBoost model were compared on Q1
and independent Q3 control samples. Factor-rank correlations are 0.9962 for
formal adverse status and 0.9938 for early deterioration; the top-ten overlap
is 10 and 9, respectively. This indicates high explanation stability within the
studied cohorts, but does not establish causality or replace external validation
on another source.
