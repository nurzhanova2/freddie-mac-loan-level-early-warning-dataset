# Chapter 6. Results, discussion and conclusions

## 6.1. Purpose of the chapter

This chapter will contain descriptive statistics, outcome rates, model comparison, calibration, lead time, false alarms and explanation analysis. Model results have not yet been calculated; numerical conclusions are therefore intentionally not presented.

## 6.2. Limitations

Conclusions will be limited to the Fannie Mae Primary Dataset and the 2008Q1 cohort until further vintages are added. External application to Freddie Mac requires a separate harmonisation of fields and outcomes. Observational associations and SHAP values do not prove causation.

## 6.3. Status

The chapter is a dissertation-ready framework and will be completed after labels, model training, out-of-time validation and explainability analysis.
